## Typography
- Display: 'Clash Display', 'Satoshi', sans-serif
- Body: 'Inter', 'Poppins', sans-serif
- Italic Highlight: 'Playfair Display', serif (Italic 500)

## Posture Rules
- Luxury, Elite, Corporate, Futuristic.
- Glassmorphism cards with gold glowing accents.
- Smooth scrolling and cinematic transitions.
- Atelier Zero structural elements (Side rails, Roman numerals).
